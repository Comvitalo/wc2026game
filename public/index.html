How points work

Match tips

For every match you predict the exact score (e.g. 3:2). When the match kicks off your tip locks and is compared to the real result:

Your tip vs. the real resultPoints
Exact result — you got the score spot on (tip 3:2, result 3:2)5
Correct goal difference — right margin, wrong score (tip 2:1, result 3:2)3
Correct tendency — right winner or a draw, but wrong margin1
Wrong — wrong winner / predicted the wrong outcome0

A correct goal difference always counts as the higher score, not just a tendency. Two different draws (tip 1:1, result 2:2) count as a correct goal difference.

Bonus questions

Season-long predictions (winner, semi-finalists, top scorer, …). Each question shows its own points value on the Bonus tab. For multi-answer questions (e.g. the four semi-finalists, 2 points each) you earn the points for each correct pick. Bonus answers lock at the tournament start.

Privacy & locking

Your tips and bonus answers are private — nobody else can see them. Each match tip locks at that match's kickoff; at that moment it's frozen and everyone's tips for that match are revealed. You can keep tipping later matches after the tournament has started.
